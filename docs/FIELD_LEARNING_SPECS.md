# Watched field work, gathering and mineral scouting

This continuation closes concrete gaps in I2/I3. Existing selected lessons now
accept actual player farming/nursery examples; loose-food collection uses Harvest
knowledge; Scouting is a fifth personal routine. The current Hearth household and
remembered delivery routes remain intact. Broader professions, citizen care,
combat tactics and civilization are still unfinished.

## A lesson can begin with an example

Choose the existing Teach/Guide action in Work → Lessons. The conversation closes.
An eligible animal can learn by watching its actual instructor, as well as by
performing existing guided work. There is no new training item, control bank,
stat dump or persistent tutorial. The existing practice/learned feedback confirms
real experience.

| Selected routine | Successful player example |
| --- | --- |
| Harvest | Break a ripe supported plant with actual matching food drops, or pick ripe fruit/berries and receive real produce. Existing Diet/renewable-plant predicates are reused. |
| Garden | Plant a compatible crop or ordinary wheat/beetroot/melon seeds; pay for real bone-meal growth; or consume compatible compostable food while the composter actually advances. |
| Nursery | Pay bone meal or Starlit Dust to advance the same real litter in a Cradle. Held-item and native-menu care share the confirmation. |
| Scouting | Mine actual surveyable ore with real drops while the pupil watches. Showing an ingot, inspecting a compass or opening a menu grants nothing. |

Both actors must be local, safe and authorized; the pupil must see the action
and instructor. Harvest/Garden/Nursery retain home, daylight, stat and health
requirements. Later actual work still checks paths, stock, world permissions,
filters, redstone, Diet and food reserves.

Each pupil retains at most one transient pending example. Native events identify
the actual player, hand, original state and drops. The following tick confirms
the world outcome and payment/produce. Cancelled events, failed planting, immature
harvests, unchanged growth, passive incubation, occlusion, lost authority,
interruptions and reload do not invent experience. Ambiguous outcomes are ignored.
This is a bounded adapter for supported actions, not arbitrary third-party
transaction provenance.

Broken plants/ore need their actual spawned drops or the corresponding increase
in the player's inventory. Picked fruit needs new ground produce or a real
inventory increase. Planting/growth needs a changed plant and consumed input
(Creative still needs the world outcome). Nursery care needs the same attached
block entity, unchanged litter components and advanced age. Demonstrations earn
ordinary personal practice, never genes or Hearth production/generation receipts.
The animal must still do its own work for those progression receipts.

## Gathering is work, eating is instinct

Healthy opted-in loose-food collection needs Harvest capacity/knowledge or a
valid local lesson. The old stat-only collection Goal and read-only work census
now agree. A committed component-preserving collection earns practice and shared
rest; refused/rolled-back work earns neither. Guided work requires the instructor
to see both the loose item and its destination. Injured foraging, ordinary meals,
affection, transport and trusted recall remain available to limited minds.

## A field scout is personally taught

Scouting supported capacities are **1 / .75 / .5 / .75**, and independent
capacities **1.75 / 1.5 / .75 / 1.25**, in Memory/Reasoning/Handling/Communication
order. All contributing gene copies use the existing pure rules/forecasts. Bat
ears and Senses +10 remain physical requirements; focused surveys need Senses +20.

Field lessons need neither a Nest nor daylight. The chosen scout accompanies
its instructor through safe loaded paths; fear, combat, conversations and explicit
orders retain priority. The helper profile needs two real examples; stronger
retention/communication can learn sooner. A supported mind still needs its active
authorized guide after learning. The initial Hearth teacher planner retains its
four household routines rather than claiming a geological field curriculum.

Only an educated, capable and currently eligible scout performs a compass survey.
The existing six-block loaded scan, focused ore families, half-health requirement,
native compass splitting/components and thirty-second recovery remain, including
empty surveys. A lead does not prevent this local stationary survey. Other
keepers cannot obtain a private survey. Refusals/inspection never scan, spend a
compass, start recovery or award exploration milestones. Requested Calipers/
Book/Table mind pages now include Scouting; conversations do not reveal vectors.

## Saves, evidence and remaining scope

Protocol **35** requires matching clients/servers. Genome/seed allele order,
samples, item components and route formats do not change. Personal practice is
`Learning:"l2.h-d-g-n-s"`, five bounded 0–6 values with the original four positions
unchanged. Old l1 adults retain those exact four values and gain familiar surveying;
old pups retain the four values without adult scouting experience. New animals
start without Scouting lessons. Future learning text stays opaque. Pending
examples and instructor sessions are never serialized.

Native scenarios use actual lesson buttons, crop breaks, native seed planting/
bone-meal use, held-item/native-menu nursery care, ore mining, compass surveys and
loose-food work. They cover cancellation, immature/occluded examples, pending
reload, supported/limited minds, legacy adult/pup migration and exact stock.
The ordinary cognition/localization gates cover schema, inheritance and expanded
mind-page bounds. First-person discovery, graphical appearance and natural pacing
remain player review.

Paid household wound care and learned group tactics now ship in
[CARE_AND_TACTICS_SPECS.md](CARE_AND_TACTICS_SPECS.md). Next: shared procedures/
route teaching and professions. Arbitrary machines, multi-stop programs, full
civic downed rescue and a fully autonomous wild society remain unfinished.
