# Original household cargo after carrier loss

Household work now keeps the original public parcel identifiable when its carrier
dies. The existing native `LivingDropsEvent` receives one real item entity from
the original carried stack, with its saved site and original parcel owner. This
uses saved membership, without loading or resolving a distant Hearth. Repeated
drop processing cannot emit the cargo twice. The finite source stays spent;
ordinary item despawning, fire, pickup delay and loaded terrain still apply.

A qualified resident can collect that real owner/site-marked supply near the
existing permitted work plots, walk it back and return it through the normal
authority, native protection and exact inventory transaction checks. Transferring
the Hearth does not transfer an old owner's outstanding parcel. Restoring that
authority makes the original saved item eligible again. Finding or carrying an
item teaches no routine and grants no body points.

## Shared worn tools and replacement bills

Ordinary wooden axes and pickaxes remain public tools with native damage. Gear
with names, enchantments or other private custom components stays excluded even
if its owner/site markers match. The existing occupied rescue basket keeps its
exact private passenger data and manual recovery route; a cargo drop neither
resurrects nor releases that animal.

A missing usable tool's actual ground recovery takes priority over making a new
one. A genuinely hungry resident may recover the real missing axe when it is
the link to ripe orchard food, then return it, prune with one normal wear and
pay an actual meal. Optional pickaxe recovery still observes family preparation
and ration policy.

Only an actual started eligible pickup or an original owned tool on an available
carrier can postpone a replacement bill. Reading offers reserves nothing;
canceling a pickup, losing its source/role or encountering existing path backoff
releases that demand. The ordinary eighty-tick work cooldown still counts as
real custody. An injured, paused or otherwise unsafe carrier cannot indefinitely
freeze a healthy craftsperson. The original gear stays aboard while an eligible
replacement remains an ordinary paid recipe, without repair or duplication.

## Physical cooperation at the first cache

The cache's full collision shape must still be clear after placement callbacks.
An idle household resident may stand legally beside the small marker while
overlapping that future shape. When a real paid cache is already carried or
available on the work mat, an otherwise idle, calm resident can walk to a nearby
safe stand clear of the future cache. It uses native reachable paths and loaded
dry standing cells, with the existing bounded movement deadline.

This fallback claims no resource job, pays no ration and teaches no skill. It
respects root power/locks, safety, explicit lessons, courier/apprentice activity
and higher-priority movement. It stops once its actual body clears. It cannot
force another body aside, teleport it or consume the cache while blocked.

## Evidence and remaining acceptance

Five focused native recovery cases exercise original finite timber, owner/site
transfer and item saves, exact worn pickaxe custody, injured-carrier replacement
availability, started/canceled worn-axe recovery, private gear and an actual
autonomous pickup/return. The death-drop cases replay the native event handler
and save/reload the resulting actual item entity; they are not a complete combat
death simulation. The autonomous case removes the original carrier after that
drop, and the surviving zero-point resident walks both legs for one worn axe.
Existing rescue cases separately preserve the occupied basket.

The idle-body cache case reproduces the missing movement opportunity before the
fallback and then enables intact AI to prove an actual walk, the same paid cache
installation, unchanged health and no learned routine/body points. It also checks
the real redstone pause. These are controlled functional observations, separate
from natural attrition, client/co-op appearance, pacing and multiple generations.

Continue testing role loss and redundant practical education with genuine paid
work. Recoverable materials alone cannot replace a lost specialist's knowledge,
and no new founder, copied skill, immortal floor store or rerolled gene is granted.
