# Wild family glades — paid primitive workshops and first Nests

This returns to the original Generations direction: exploring can reveal a family
whose inherited bodies and minds affect how it actually lives. This is the first
natural bootstrap, not a claim of complete civilization or independent trade.

## A new natural site

A registered feature attempts placement in new Plains/Sunflower Plains terrain,
one in sixteen chunks before fit/biome checks. It requires a flat 15×19 dirt-based
glade, loaded generation neighbors and four clear/replaceable-plant layers. It
never clears a tree trunk, block entity, solid obstruction or water. A failed preflight
changes nothing. It will not activate an older warren or player Hearth.

The finite starting landscape contains an **empty** common root cache (the
existing Hearth Tree), three fallen guava logs, three hanging fruit,
four mature Coppices, three mature wheat plants and a separate farmland nursery.
Fresh glades also contain two small natural stone outcrops (twelve original
blocks) and empty, bounded plots for a native crafting table, three Nests, a
furnace and four supported torches. No table, Nest, tool, cobblestone stock,
furnace, charcoal or lights are supplied.
The third rest plot sits beside the central building approach. Old saves retain
their original Nest coordinates and original permissions; their original
room can now use a bounded loaded walking detour around the old central Nest.
This changes no old blocks or approved room boundaries.

These natural starting objects are provided by generation. Residents must now
craft, carry and place their initial Nests, using the native three-wheat and
three-plank recipe. The common root cache remains generated infrastructure;
construction of that primitive cache from raw materials remains open.
There is no stocked inventory, tool, glass Cradle,
metal-workshop grant, instant food income or progression receipt.

Three normal Meadow founder genomes are frozen once in the original detached
block entity. No selection/retry for intelligence, high stats, or useful Diet.
All founders begin with zero bred points and empty individual learning. Their
actual minds may leave the family primitive or missing a needed specialist.
Each founder has one persisted spawn attempt and a stable site-derived UUID;
death or native spawn cancellation never supplies replacement residents.

The site has its own community identity. A visitor cannot claim its store,
confiscate carried goods through the recovery control or dismiss residents.
This does not make the terrain indestructible. Respect existing block protection,
redstone pauses and native world interactions; adoption/trade are later systems.

## First real work

Only residents of this natural site may independently discover primitive chores,
and only with the routine's existing independent genetic capacity. Membership
alone gives no skill; limited minds remain limited. Unknown future learning data
cannot be overwritten. Successful work earns ordinary saved personal practice.
Player-established households retain their existing learning/stat requirements.

- Harvesters spend a longer visible work period clipping the three known fallen
  logs, or pick the actual hanging ripe fruit. Used-log permissions persist:
  placing another log there never authorizes demolition of a player structure.
- Output is real household-marked ground stock, with ordinary pickup delay,
  lifetime and protection. It is not teleported into the cache.
- A capable beginner can carry **one** marked item home without the advanced
  Senses requirement. Existing trained/bred bulk haulers retain their advantage.
- Primitive crafting is limited to the existing native Planks, Sticks, Wooden
  Crafting Table, Axe/Pickaxe, Furnace, Torch, Nest and Coppice recipes, with real exact inputs,
  outputs and remainders.
  Native recipes still determine affordability. No free grafting frames,
  dressings or incense knowledge.
  Successful recipes now practice distinct Crafting, and actual paid placement
  practices Building; they no longer silently train Gardening. See
  [WORKSHOP_LEARNING_SPECS.md](WORKSHOP_LEARNING_SPECS.md). Primitive body/capacity
  exceptions, original materials and the six-operation ration budget remain.
- The paid axe unlocks normal Coppice pruning. Real grain crops yield native
  drops and spend a returned planting item to replant. That actual successful
  replanting also practices Gardening for a natural independently capable
  pioneer; ordinary harvest, a canceled operation or a recipe grants no such
  experience. Actual wheat makes the first and additional Nests; personal Diet
  still controls resident meals.
- Initial rest plots use the existing paid construction parcels, original site
  and owner, native callbacks and actual placement receipts. A builder does not
  need a personal home before making its first Nest. Removed Nests are not
  automatically rebuilt. Missing `PrimitiveRestPlots` in either earlier glade
  revision means no new rest permission; loading clears cached current cells
  before resolving the original saved plots. Old beds and stock remain exact.
- Existing carried construction parcels and inherited building capacity apply
  to the reserved room south of the cache. A broad founder body produces a
  three-wide entrance instead of a single-wide entrance. No new land is silently
  approved outside the original glade; removed walls still need repair consent.

Shared claims, physical travel, safety AI, ration/rest budgets, public need plans
and actual world permissions retain priority. Advanced growth, medical care,
grafting and ordinary store routes do not gain a zero-stat shortcut.

## A physical first workbench

Standard primitive furniture now belongs to learned Crafting in new workbench
glades. A craftsperson qualifies only after personally learning Crafting;
qualified builders retain their original path. The craftsperson may assemble
only the original nine table/furnace/light/Nest plots. It uses the same
real fetched material, native placement callbacks and ownership receipts. This
exception supplies no new land and does not extend to rooms, walls, roofs,
annexes or general repairs. Those still require Building. A craft-only assembler
gains no Building experience, and a limiting splice still refuses placement and
returns its original parcel safely.

The [founder audit](audits/founder-role-coverage.md) found an architect in only
1.94% of 100,000 sampled three-founder groups, versus a craftsperson in 65.234%.
Requiring the architect for first bedding blocked families before they could
breed one. The corrected task split preserves the original founder weights and
all inherited capacity thresholds; it changes the complexity required for
standard furniture, without granting experience or ignoring genetic limits.
Three native `primitive_*` cases additionally pass with the fixed ordinary
helper profile `(2.25, 2.0, 1.75, 2.0)`, zero bred points and empty starting
learning. They cover actual first-table/Nest costs, excluded room work, no
Building progress, missing experience, a limiting callback with parcel recovery
and native autonomous first tools/claimed bedding. The complete Java 25
build/model/localization and all **315** native cases pass. This controlled
reference is separate from random-founder survival or long-term continuity.

Fresh glades now require an actual native Crafting Table for recipes wider or
taller than two slots. Making the table itself needs four actual planks in a
two-by-two recipe, so it does not require a table. The three original fallen
logs provide twelve planks: four for the table, two for four shared sticks,
three for the axe and three for the pick. Real walking, work and meal budgets
still apply; this arithmetic is not an elapsed-time or free-stock grant.

A builder fetches the real table, carries it to its original plot two blocks
west of the root and places it through the existing native protection/receipt
path. Stock alone supplies no workspace. Larger recipes require actual loaded,
unpowered table presence, with stock and table both within the animal's reach
and sight. The movement goal chooses a real working position for both targets
and looks at the physical table. Permission callbacks revalidate that workspace
before native ingredient/result/remainder commit. A player guide or observing
resident must also see the table to teach those larger recipes.

The plot is saved only in new `PrimitiveWorkbenchPlot` sites. Missing that flag
preserves all earlier glade/household crafting rules and original plots; it adds
no table, land or new dependency to old saves. Loading clears the cached plot
list before resolving that saved revision. A table supplied on the already
authorized plot provides workspace, without giving construction receipts or
skills. Deliberately removed crew-built tables are not silently rebuilt.

Local actual construction parcels count toward workshop needs. A walking table
prevents spending the final four planks on a spare; a walking furnace prevents
another furnace/quarry bill; stored torches plus their actual carried parcels
satisfy the original four light plots. These are original saved materials in
real local bodies, not reservations created by viewing offers or virtual stock.
Returned parcels still recover through the same original inventory journal.
Three native workbench cases cover the paid native table, callback/power loss,
actual two-target observation, cached old-revision isolation and multi-worker
table/furnace/torch handoffs through saves. The complete Java 25 build/model/
localization and all **312** native cases pass for this checkpoint. Autonomous
native goals also complete the paid table/furnace/lights and first claimed Nest
with actual growth/rations/night rest; current observations are in the appendix.

## Stone, a native furnace and actual light

The finite stone permission belongs only to the twelve generated cells of a new
site. A miner fetches one actual shared Wooden Pickaxe, carries it to exposed
stone and uses the native stone loot with that tool. Each success wears the
actual carried pickaxe. Paid tools survive native animal saves, return to the
original common store and drop once on death. Named/private tools remain
excluded; normal wear does not make a shared tool unusable to the crew.

Cobblestone becomes real marked ground output. A hauler must collect it and walk
it home before the actual eight-cobblestone Furnace recipe is affordable. A
builder then carries that real furnace to the original supported plot. Only the
crew's own successful, empty new furnace is linked for operation. There is no
remote mining credit or automatic ownership of a replacement container.

Primitive workers can supply their furnace with real logs and wood fuel,
following the native workshop path in [HOUSEHOLD_COMMONS_SPECS.md](HOUSEHOLD_COMMONS_SPECS.md).
Native loaded cooking makes charcoal; a courier carries it home, and the native
charcoal/stick recipe pays for four torches. Builders carry and place each light
on its actual supported plot. Metal/glass/food processing still needs ordinary
learned/physical qualifications rather than primitive membership alone.

Quarrying never excavates arbitrary terrain. Protection/cancellation and callback
revalidation precede payment; deposits with obstructions above them wait. Spent
cell permissions survive saves and cannot be renewed by placing another stone.
Observed lost originals retire conservatively. The existing loaded-site index
performs bounded observation; it never acquires a missing chunk. Player-removed
finished workshop cells retain the usual deliberate-repair rule, while confirmed
explosion losses use the existing paid repair receipts.

Real dependencies come ahead of surplus room materials. The first workshop keeps
a twelve-plank preparation target/reserve, prices its own sticks ahead of roof
surplus and leaves actual instructions through the existing capable planner.
The reserve applies only to new glades with unfinished workshop cells. It grants
no stock, role, skill or resource. The original six-operation ration budget still
applies, so the family can exhaust its first harvest and wait for actual fruit/
coppice regrowth. Healthy idle adults do not gain a new starvation timer.

## A soil nursery, not a glass prerequisite

An independently capable primitive carer can pay two actual guavas when there
are two ready, housed parents, enough food and room for another generation.
The existing courtship produces the actual parental seed. Another in-flight
seed, existing pups or occupied nursery prevents repeated family initiation.

The carer walks that exact seed to the reserved farmland and plants a real Seed
Crop, preserving its full components. It grows normally; zero Bloom does not
accelerate it. At maturity the carer uses the existing safe nursery pad/crowding
and native spawn checks. Pups inherit the family home and genes, never education.

The crop reserves its seed before spawn callbacks. Cancellation rolls back pups
and returns the same ripe seed; removal returns the one exact item instead.
Re-entry cannot hatch the same seed twice. Carried stock and original ownership
survive ordinary native saves/unloads; unloaded work pauses without forcing
terrain. A wild glade cannot gift the player's Living Keystone progression.

## Evidence and remaining work

The original eight native scenarios cover detached WorldGenRegion/ProtoChunk configuration
and native saves; empty genesis/private stores/no founder respawn or spawn-veto retry; physical
fallen-wood hauling and native tool/material recipes; actual parental courtship,
soil planting and membership-bearing birth; native spawn refusal/retry; inherited
limits/old-site separation; and actual unsupervised goal-driven first-tool work.
The paid nursery scenario supplies its food fixture; the materials scenario
separately exercises real harvesting/hauling. Natural long-term pacing is not
established by a shortened goal test.

Four additional `wild_works_*` native cases cover the empty-stock tool → stone →
furnace → charcoal → light chain, native tool custody/wear/saves, permission and
callback refusals, spent-deposit replay, old-save isolation and actual unsupervised
completion. The controlled work chain provisions no materials beyond the generated
landscape. Its selected capable founders are a test condition, not a new natural
founder selection rule.

One autonomous native observation completed after **25,378 loaded ticks** (about
21.1 minutes at the normal 20 TPS). It included native fruit/coppice growth and
the original meal payments, with zero guavas left. The native test server executes
those ticks faster than wall time. This is one controlled functional observation,
not a measured Survival rate or a guarantee for random founder minds. Slow crop
growth, actual loaded waiting and missing specialists remain meaningful.
The full 281-case native gate also completed this chain in 26,126 loaded ticks.
Exact recorded checkpoints are retained in
[`audits/wild-workshop-observations.json`](audits/wild-workshop-observations.json).

`art/wild_glade/overview.png` maps the actual detached generation snapshot and
the same controlled site before/after work. Its adjacent JSON/provenance and
`art/preview_wild_glade.py` reproduce the schematic. It is block-position evidence,
not client geometry, texture, animation or lighting acceptance.

Initial Nests are now paid resident construction. The controlled chain proves
three original Nest recipes, exact ingredient payments, actual parcels and
owned placement. Its crop age is explicitly advanced for transaction checks;
the autonomous scenario retains native crop growth, food payments, night rest
and walking, and proves a first built-and-claimed bed plus the furnace/lights.
Those are distinct evidence scopes. An exploratory requirement for all three
beds was still incomplete after 120,000 loaded ticks (two beds); it is not
reported as a passing multi-generation settlement. New rest plots, native
harvest/replant practice, canceled placement and both older save revisions have
dedicated native checks. Current validation is recorded in `../START_HERE.md`.
Java 25 build/model/localization and all fourteen focused wild cases pass. The
first claimed paid bed/workshop was observed in 29,962 and 72,961 loaded ticks;
these include native waiting and are not player-effort estimates. The observation
appendix retains the failed larger exploratory goal alongside those successes.

Still unfinished: self-made initial cache, broader physical recipe/build culture
and records, complete essential-specialist redundancy,
composed public works, adoption/relationships and independent
inter-community exchange. Glade frequency, survival over multiple generations,

In particular, observe table crowding/sight and real food/wood waits with naturally
rolled founders. The flat-site native fixtures do not establish a self-sustaining
population or general client navigation feel.
