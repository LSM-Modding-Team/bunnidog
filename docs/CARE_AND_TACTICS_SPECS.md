# Household wound care and learned group tactics

This continuation integrates two remaining I3 journeys with the focused Hearth
household and chosen combat party. It adds actual paid treatment, personal combat
experience and gene-dependent coordination. Full citizen downed rescue, clinics,
public records, broader professions and autonomous wild civilization remain
future work. Ordinary Apex combat numbers are unchanged.

## Supplies, a resting friend and an actual carer

Hearth carers now use the existing Hearth Poultice to treat quiet wounds.
Autonomous care spends one dressing only after native healing permission allows
actual treatment. It restores four HP (two hearts), subject to the native healing
event, and starts a saved ten-second interval for that patient. Manual poultices
retain their existing six-HP ally heal, five-second player cooldown and successful
hunt-support credit. Observation attaches to that actual accepted manual heal.
There is no breeding, forced growth, injury reset or instant revive. Ordinary
hand food, bonded first aid and recall retain their contracts for every mind.

The patient must be alive, on safe dry ground, outside combat/refuge/transport,
free of explicit travel orders and sufficiently quiet after recent attacks. A
downed bonded companion still needs its original guava first aid. Dressing care
does not clear the companion's existing recovery time. Refusals keep the supplies.
Repeated clicking, a cancelled native heal and stale callbacks grant no benefit.

Hearth carers use the existing Nursery capacity/skill, now labeled **Family care**,
and Bloom +20. Its saved ID and learned progress do not change. A capable
independent resident can work alone; a supported learner needs its actual local
teacher/mentor. During a Family care lesson, watching real paid manual
treatment teaches the same family-care routine as Cradle work. No new learning
schema or separate lesson bank is introduced. Player demonstrations never count
as the offspring's own independent work or as produced Hearth supplies.

A carer notices a visible injured member of its own household, resting within
three blocks of that member's actual claimed Nest. Other keepers' animals,
strangers, travelling residents and combat targets are excluded. The carer checks
both walking legs, approaches the Hearth, collects one exact dressing, walks to
the patient and performs treatment at close range with sight. It checks the
patient's UUID, original Nest, household/owner, materials and physical conditions
again before treatment. Both collection and treatment honor the native
mob-griefing protection event; redstone at the Hearth pauses them.

The dressing remains real saved Society cargo in transit. Original container
journals move it from stock; its custom components are retained. Native healing
callbacks run before payment and the final health change, with reentry guards
and full revalidation. If the patient leaves or becomes ineligible, the unused
dressing returns physically to the original Hearth. A paused worker keeps it.
Missing/changed source ownership does not authorize a different store. The
original owner can recover stranded medical supplies through Cargo, including
after source destruction; a full player inventory leaves the remainder owned.
Ordinary death uses the existing Society cargo drop once.

Where stock is missing, a capable Garden craftsperson can respond to a visible
care need by making the existing four-dressing recipe: one ordinary guava, bone
meal, paper and honeycomb. At least eight guavas remain. Those inputs must really
be present; this is no automatic honey/paper farm. Care and essential crafting
preempt expansion work, within the existing work/meal budget.

## Fighting experience above a reliable baseline

Fresh fighters retain basic Assist, direct focus, held defense, the normal heard
Whistle, safety warnings and bodily bite/speed/resilience. They initially choose
their immediate legal threats by instinct. They learn group tactics through
successful native bites in real exchanges involving their visible, participating
keeper. Cancelled/zero-damage hits, ordinary carrying and waiting grant nothing.

Personal `CombatTactics` practice is bounded 0–6, using the same memory plus
communication learning gain as ordinary lessons. A helper normally needs two
successful exchanges. Mastered experience does not improve genes or bite stats.
Tactics supported capacities are **1.25 / 1 / .75 / 1**; independent capacities
are **2 / 1.75 / 1.25 / 1.75**, in Memory/Reasoning/Handling/Communication order.
The existing multiple-gene contribution/forecast rules evaluate them jointly.

Educated capable fighters distribute effort across visible legal threats instead
of all choosing the nearest one. They can also respond to a recent attack on a
visible chosen partner, retaining temperament distinctions. This stays within the
existing three-member roster, keeper/hearing bounds, safe paths, pursuit limits
and target exclusions. No carrier is recruited implicitly, and a warning does
not reveal or target enemies through walls. Explicit player focus remains direct.

Supported tactical minds need a recent real combat cue from a keeper within sight
and hearing. Leaving that range suspends coordination; instinctive fighting and
recall remain dependable. A limiting splice makes experience dormant rather than
erasing it. Samples, seeds and offspring inherit capacity, never this experience.
The requested Calipers Combat reading shows a short current learning descriptor;
the Mind/forecast pages show actual capacity/odds. No combat-training menu or
persistent tutorial is added.
