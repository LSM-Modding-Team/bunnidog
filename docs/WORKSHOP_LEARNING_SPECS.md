# Practical craft and construction education

Crafting and building now have their own personal routines. Gardening no longer
implicitly trains a new carpenter or builder. Inherited capacity, body readiness,
actual learned work, material stock and authority remain separate requirements.
This advances the education/specialist-replacement gaps in
[COMPLETION_AUDIT.md](COMPLETION_AUDIT.md); general recipe culture and full wild
settlement continuity remain unfinished.

## Real paid lessons

A registered resident's **Work → Lessons → Workshop lessons** offers Crafting
and Building. Starting a valid lesson closes the real conversation. The existing
Society goal walks to the actual work front, preserving one movement owner.
Searching, approaching, working, missing guide and the real eighty-tick household
rest are reported when the player requests the Work/Lessons view.

Workshop practice uses the household's existing body/safety conditions and
Bloom +10, within its original loaded local footprint. It does not require a
personal Nest or prior gardening lessons: requiring a completed home before
learning to build the first Nest would create another bootstrap circle. Normal
night/home rest, danger, orders, injury, stock, redstone and native permissions
still constrain work. Natural zero-point pioneers keep their separate bounded
primitive-discovery route; this is not a new Bloom gate on their first workshop.

The instructor must remain authorized, nearby and visible, and see both work
targets. A lesson focuses its routine; unrelated civic work cannot consume that
practice window. Necessary meals and physical return of existing owned parcels
remain possible, including after the instructor leaves. Recall, splicing,
unsafe state, departure, reload and the bounded session preserve the same
interruption rules as other lessons. Knowledge stays personal and saved.

- A completed native recipe earns Crafting practice after its exact ingredient,
  result and remainder transaction commits. Native furnace input/fuel delivery
  also practices Crafting after its real parcel is inserted into the original
  linked container. Collection, a failed transfer or waiting for a timer earns none.
- Building practice requires actual paid placement and the original construction
  receipt. Fetching a block grants no skill. The original carried material,
  protection/callback guards, physical return and owner recovery remain.
- Opening a menu, submitting a plan or the old timed resident lecture cannot
  grant either workshop routine. No new recipe, material, skill or project
  permission appears from a high-capacity mind alone.

Native crafting rechecks capacity, the current guide and original household
after permission callbacks and before transaction commit. A callback that removes
the instructor cannot spend the ingredients for that abandoned lesson. Exact
keepsakes, native inventory journals and reentrant-operation guards remain.

## Residents can train a real replacement

A locally visible, calm, qualified same-household craftsperson can guide a
beginner through actual paid work. The existing six-block teaching range and
independent Teaching capacity apply. A construction foreman retains the existing
twelve-block practical supervision range and must personally know independent
Building; a foreman does not need a separate Teaching qualification merely to
supervise a block placement.

Successful supervised work advances the pupil's corresponding routine. A pupil
with independent hereditary potential can then work after its original expert
is gone. A supported mind can know the routine while still needing local help;
a limited mind cannot acquire or perform it through repetition. New workshop
skills do not upload Gardening or one another. The existing personal practice
gain, complementary capacities and physical stats remain meaningful.

Crafting potential explicitly combines memory, reasoning, handling and
communication. Its independent thresholds preserve the former practical
gardener/craftsperson capacity, while supported crafting also requires the old
handling/reasoning minima. Building retains its existing stricter four-axis
capacity rule. No body stat, gene order or inheritance law changes.

## Bounded menus and deliberate measurement

Workshop lessons occupy their own short choice page, with the usual Back/Close
history and brief live state. This preserves the native twelve-control limit,
including an animal with an existing delivery route. It is education, not a
permanent profession selector or a tutorial panel.

Requested Calipers readings gain a separate **Workshops** purpose. The existing
Work purpose retains its original routines and body requirements within twelve
rows. Actual mind/sample/seed/Table pages include Crafting and Building capacity
and exact joint offspring odds; their eight rows fit the existing two-page
reader. The header hovers retain training/purity explanations. These are tool
measurements, never unsolicited statistics in animal conversations.

English and the shared neutral Spanish source cover all new text. Network
**51** requires matching client/server content. The exported native pure page
preview in `art/workshop_learning/` is headless layout evidence; actual client
fonts, controls and feel remain human review.

## Save compatibility

Personal learning now writes `l3.h-d-g-n-s-c-b`, seven bounded 0–6 values in the
same integer, appending Crafting and Building after the five original routines.
The first five slots, existing button IDs, genotype and genetic snapshots stay
unchanged. No learned skill enters an offspring, tissue, seed or infusion.

Old l1/l2 **adult** Garden masters retain Crafting/Building mastery once because
those exact jobs previously used Garden knowledge. All original progress stays
exact. Old beginners do not gain workshop mastery; old pups retain their former
progress without the added adult experience. Explicit l3 records never repeat
this compatibility grant. Legacy body-only adults without a learning field keep
their familiar repertoire as before. Unreadable future learning records remain
opaque, unmodified and ineligible for work.
