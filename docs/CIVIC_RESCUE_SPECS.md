# Local civic defeat and physical rescue

Registered adult residents with an actual claimed local Nest can now survive
ordinary combat defeat and be carried home by a competent housemate. This closes
the local pickup/transport gap in the accepted intelligence roadmap. It uses
existing native Travel Baskets, actual household stock, learned Family care and
the existing first-aid/recovery rules. No new screen or combat recruitment is
required.

## Who becomes a patient

The citizen must belong to an attached original Hearth, remain within its
24-block household bounds and have a surviving claimed Nest within that same
area. An existing keeper bond must agree with the Hearth owner. Babies,
unregistered wild animals, foreign bonds and explicitly travelling citizens do
not gain this civic protection. Natural households retain their own community
authority; they do not require a player bond.

A lethal native combat hit leaves the eligible adult alive at one HP, downed
and injured through the existing combat state. It stops fighting, walking and
working. Ordinary subsequent combat hits cannot repeatedly kill that downed
citizen. Deliberate player harm to an unbonded citizen and ordinary environmental
hazards can still kill it. Existing bonded-companion damage/death rules remain.
Genes, bred points, education and party membership are not granted or reset.
Loaded ticking and saved native injury state remain authoritative.

## An actual rescue journey

A carer needs the existing learned Family care routine, its real multiple-gene
capacity/mentor rules and Bloom +20. A primitive discovery permission alone does
not grant advanced rescue. A quiet same-household patient must be grounded, dry,
visible and out of danger, with no leash/passengers, travel order or conflicting
transport. Native protection and safe path checks apply. The original patient
UUID, Nest, household site and source owner are saved in its carer's parcel.

At a Nest, the carer can already fetch and deliver one actual guava for first
aid. For a patient farther than three blocks from its Nest, the carer instead:

1. Walks to the original Hearth and withdraws one real ordinary empty Travel
   Basket through the native inventory journal. Named or occupied baskets are
   excluded. Both the patient and recovery area must be physically reachable.
2. Walks to that same quiet patient. Native basket capture stores the entire
   actual animal, including its UUID, genes, points, education, equipment,
   residency, injuries and any existing carried goods. The body leaves the
   world; no second animal or virtual patient is created. Standard capture
   releases its old native Nest ticket.
3. Walks the occupied basket to a safe loaded standing space at the original
   Nest and uses normal native basket release. A successful release reclaims
   the still-free original Nest through its actual POI ticket. Household members'
   automatic Nest searches respect the saved in-flight rescue destination.
   Explicit player reassignment and other actual interference can still block it.
4. Walks the reusable empty basket back to the original stock. The citizen
   remains downed at one HP until separate paid first aid succeeds. That payment
   restores mobility while retaining injury recovery, rather than providing a
   free heal or resurrection during transport.

Started work claims and saved medical parcels prevent another loaded housemate
from spending supplies on the same patient. The existing work-shift ration and
rest intervals apply. Rescue jobs preempt ordinary expansion; full carers still
need meals. There is no remote pickup, teleporting supply credit or forced chunk
loading. These parcels use the original household's authority, not an arbitrary
same-coordinate replacement Hearth.

## Material handoff and interruption

A qualified learned craftsperson can respond to a real unmet rescue need with
the existing native basket recipe: six planks, one Leather and one Lead. All
ingredients must actually be donated/present; none are manufactured by the
planner. A capable planner may record that need and its plank/basket dependencies
for another worker. The recipe is not added to primitive discovery, and this
does not implement a wild Leather/Lead supply chain.

Native griefing permission and exact original patient, authority, cargo and
location checks run before payment/capture/release. During native capture or
spawn/removal callbacks the actual basket is held outside externally recoverable
cargo. Refused spawns, moved patients and reentrant retrieval/death notifications
cannot turn that operation into a duplicate body or basket. Native failed release
keeps its occupied basket intact.

Locks, redstone, unsafe/unloaded spaces and a paused/unsafe carer retain the real
parcel. Lost skills return an unused basket. If the recovery space is blocked,
the Nest is claimed by someone else or the destination becomes invalid, the
occupied basket can return to the original Hearth for manual recovery. It is
not emptied into an unsafe place. Losing/destroying the source pauses autonomous
work; the original owner can still recover its exact supplies through Cargo.
A full owner inventory keeps the basket aboard. Carrier death drops the one
remaining actual basket once. Ordinary manual basket release can recover that
same downed citizen, including after source loss.

This is local loaded-area civic care. Broader clinics, rescue expeditions,
self-sufficient primitive basket materials, guaranteed cross-chunk crash
atomicity and natural multi-generation/client/co-op acceptance remain separate
work. A blocked patient's preservation in a basket does not count as completed
care or community continuity.

## Evidence

Seven native `rescue_*` scenarios cover actual lethal combat, native save/load,
ordinary death boundaries, exact basket recipe payment, public dependencies,
the complete physical parcel/body/first-aid chain, moved patients, native griefing
and spawn cancellation, reentrant death/recovery, blocked recovery spaces,
redstone, carrier death, limiting splices, source loss, foreign-owner/full-inventory
refusals and autonomous native walking through the whole journey. The autonomous
case retains actual quiet waiting and the ordinary meal/work rules.

Java 25 build/localization and the seven focused cases pass. The subsequent
rebalance release gate also passes all **294** required native cases, including
the rescue chain and corrected older readiness/timing fixtures. These are functional
native-server fixtures; they do not establish natural Survival timing, client
appearance, multiplayer pacing or that the entire civilization roadmap is done.
