# Playing Bunnidogs

Start with empty hands on an animal or care station. The live panel shows the
current needs, a useful next step and named controls. Crosshair hints explain
what a held item will do. Detailed genetics and the Field Guide are optional.

Minecraft's language setting selects the mod text too. All Spanish variants use
the same neutral Peruvian Spanish. Guide pages show short previews; hover a
chapter's text for its complete explanation.

## Adventure together

Train any healthy adult through care with a Trail Whistle and one plain guava.
Choose **Orders & combat → Join combat party** for up to three deliberate
fighters. Loyal partners watch for attackers, stoic partners defend close by,
feral partners suit directed strikes, and skittish partners warn and skirmish.
Existing genes and bred points change their actual abilities.

**Normal Whistle always recalls.** Crouch and aim it at an enemy to direct the
chosen partner. Crouch nearby for orders, or away for the party view. Wait with
Assist holds a small area. Ordinary carriers stay out of fights unless chosen.

A bonded adult defeated in ordinary combat collapses. Give real guava first aid
in care/orders or use an empty Travel Basket to extract it. It recuperates while
you travel or rest safely; ordinary food heals proportionally. Terrain hazards
remain dangerous. Exact rules and unfinished extensions: [combat](COMBAT_SPECS.md).

## Raise a family

- Plain guavas work for every lineage. **Care** heals or helps a pup grow;
  **Courtship** feeds a ready adult for breeding. Both spend one actual plain
  guava. Direct feeding still works; sneak-plain-guava means care.
- Feed two adults and keep them close. Mating drops a **litter seed**. Plant it
  on farmland; wait or use bone meal, then break the ripe crop to hatch pups.
- Care shows the pup's remaining loaded growth time. Golden dandelions pause
  or resume it; Bloom respects that choice. Feeding cannot add bred points.
- Nests use wheat and any planks. Each adult gets one. Lead an adult to a Nest
  and choose **Assign led adult**, or use a lead on the Nest. Nearby adults
  can claim a free Nest naturally. Empty-hand use on a led animal releases
  the lead through Minecraft's normal interaction.
- Healthy adults need no meals. Stock an optional Trough for injury recovery.
  Upgraded guavas stay reserved for breeding; saved Trough stock remains
  withdrawable. Six native milestones connect the first-session handoffs.

## Give the line a purpose

- Adults carry real vanilla bundles according to Build. Equip a bundle by using
  it on the animal; retrieve it through care or sneak-empty-hand use. Contents,
  names, colors and transport saves stay intact; excess cargo slows movement.
- Calm adults of every temperament train with a reusable **Trail Whistle** and
  one plain guava through care. Crouch-Whistle on an untrained animal opens it. Copper, two string and a feather craft the tool;
  Sinew/Whisker alternatives still work. The keeper chooses Follow, Wait or
  resident life, and can opt into ordinary meals from actual carried bundles.
- **Useful roles & work** in care shows pack capacity, temperament, actual harvest
  readiness and inherited benefits. Ordinary adults harvest suitable ripe crops
  without bred points. Specialty thresholds show what improved offspring add;
  pause/resume home work in this view or with the existing crouched hoe gesture.
- Trough panels choose Mixed, Garden, Gather, Compost, Nursery or Supply.
  Work and readiness depend on the actual animal and stock. Nursery tends
  Cradle stages with Bloom +20; Supply moves surplus with Senses +20.
  Redstone pauses resident work/meals while inventory automation remains.
- A **Commission Board** shows nine targets, your completed requests and each
  candidate requirement. Choose a request, bring a real Breeder and a parented
  adult onto its clear front block, then submit one paper. Rewards pay once;
  the animal and cargo stay. Fixed genes mean two matching copies; stats come
  from breeding, not exercise.
- At a **Genetics Table**, choose a goal and compare two reusable samples, then
  compare your actual seed at its saved quality. Calipers reveal nearby offspring
  and measure a living pup against the goal before you raise it. The actual-seed
  view finds its own offspring even when samples describe another pair.
  Tables, Boards and Pens accept ordinary feathers as well as Whiskers.

## Optional depth

Calipers measure genes, parents and stats. A Pedigree Book records real animals;
its sheets support filtering, lineage names and planning. The Table, Juicer,
Splicer and Registry support inherited lines, seed quality, infusion and stable
breeds. Empty-hand Table controls show reusable samples, place the shown next
sample without replacing stock, retrieve A/B and open the existing breeding
plans. Choose a commission goal at the Table to see combined target-gene and
point odds, required traits and the next useful step. Goals keep no animal or
sample costs; the Board still judges the actual adult. A Judging Stand first shows the led entrant's missing requirements and
five stat targets; **Claim eligible ribbon** awards once. Ribbons display the
winning snapshot on a Trophy Pedestal. Jars, displays and gene detectors now
inspect before mutation, with named sample/signal/retrieval controls. Cradles,
Boards and detectors can mark their actual working area in the world.

The optional Apex encounter previews a chosen sample and all five offerings;
Review summon followed by Summon Apex deliberately starts it. Opening never
spends materials. Ring markers show the possible Pedestal positions. Native
spawn cancellation or changed inputs retain the actual remaining stock.

Native commissions reward specialized
lines. Tool gestures and detailed care readouts remain available.

Wild origins and traits matter in the world: regional diets, home crop work,
coastal swimming/air, cave sight and surveys, terrain defenses, camouflage and
renewable Brush products. Travel Baskets preserve whole animals. Warrens,
regional kennels, abandoned barns, Show Ring arenas and Island refuges connect
exploration with supplies and founders. Native maps help find those places.

Guava timber supplies building blocks, mechanisms, signs and boats. Pelts and
materials support rugs, displays, genetics and the optional Apex challenge.
These systems extend the family loop; they are not required to hatch a litter.
