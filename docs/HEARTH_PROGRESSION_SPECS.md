# Hearth households and the six hunts

The player approved the society-first progression on 2026-10-25. This supersedes
the five-legendary-offering ritual and the single-Crest Great Warren entry rule.
It implements a small working household, not the entire civilization roadmap.
No ordinary Apex combat values change.

Current local-commons extension: [HOUSEHOLD_COMMONS_SPECS.md](HOUSEHOLD_COMMONS_SPECS.md)
adds shared claims/plans, real recipe crafting, fitted surveyed rooms, physical
building/seed parcels and material fields. Coppice outputs now await actual
hauling at the orchard, superseding the original direct-store credit. Crafting
uses shipped recipe ingredients, including native sticks/axes and incense costs.
The existing progression evidence and original site identities remain intact.

## Complete player journey

Raise useful adults, teach initial work through existing Lessons, and breed
complementary physical and cognitive capacities. Craft a Hearth Tree and
Steward's Staff from ordinary wood, guava sapling, copper and amethyst. Bind the
staff to the tree, use it on adults to invite them, and use it on nearby Troughs,
Cradles and Composters to authorize those work sites. Sneak-use on a resident
leaves the household without releasing its existing keeper/bond.

Sneak-use the staff on the tree to survey its small shelter/orchard plot; a
second use within ten seconds explicitly authorizes construction there. An
obstructed plot refuses. The world-space survey marks are temporary and requested.
The tree exposes ordinary 27-slot chest storage and one short current need on
inspection. No profession control bank, tutorial screen or permanent HUD exists.

Supply initial food, coppices, timber, bone meal and wheat as needed. Existing
work lessons/traits remain relevant: Harvest, Garden, Delivery and Nursery are
personal learned routines, and Bloom/Senses thresholds still apply. Learning is
not inherited. Capable residents automatically select useful available jobs;
less capable residents can still perform their simpler known work. There is no
required population level or title counter.

## Real work behind the Keystone

- Growers plant four authorized Guava Coppice positions. The physical plants grow
  through eight visible ages by ordinary random ticks/bonemeal. A learned
  harvester with a real wooden axe prunes a mature plant, resetting it and
  producing two real Guava Logs/four guavas. Axe wear is paid; tools can break.
- Craftspeople convert real logs to planks and make axes, grafting frames, nests
  and renewable incense. Crafting requires Garden knowledge, Bloom +10, handling
  and reasoning at least 1.5. No parallel virtual inventory exists.
- Builders need Garden knowledge and independent Building capacity. They place
  paid planks/nests into a surveyed five-by-five roofed annex south of the tree.
  There are four real nests, an open two-block entrance and a walkable interior.
  The plot never excavates terrain, replaces a chest/build or places through a
  living actor. Native mob-griefing/placement vetoes retain priority; reserved
  material is refunded if placement fails.
- Haulers need Delivery knowledge and Senses +20. They actually walk both legs
  of linked-site transfers. Exact carried items/components live in the animal's
  save between pickup and delivery, and drop on an actual death. Guava transfers
  leave the source's eight-food reserve; low troughs can be restocked from the
  Hearth. Supply and collection thresholds differ to avoid endless shuttling.
- Composters turn actual surplus guavas into real composter stages, then recover
  the bone meal from a ready composter. The family can renew its food, wood,
  wooden tools, frames and cultivation supplies after finite starter stock.
  Existing resident farming also supplies ordinary grains for further nests.
- Residents eat one stored guava after six household work jobs. Food exhaustion
  pauses their new jobs; it does not conjure meals or bypass stock ownership.
- Caretakers need Nursery knowledge/Bloom +20. With food, an empty linked Cradle
  and two eligible, actually housed adults, they pay two plain guavas and start
  native courtship. The parents produce one exact real Seed. The nurse carries
  it to the Cradle, pays to nurture it, and hatches only with a clear safe pad.
  Manual-hatch-only Cradles retain that setting. A new litter starts only below
  eight nearby household members and with no babies; native litters can have up
  to six pups, so the natural household can reach thirteen. Explicit invitation
  stops at sixteen; nothing force-loads residents or simulates unloaded births.
- Offspring remember the household identity and their origin, not adult skills.
  A resident with independent Teaching capacity and actual routine knowledge
  can approach an eligible learner. A nearby visible mentor enables real
  supported practice. Only completed useful actions earn knowledge. To prove
  continuity, a household-born offspring must master a routine with resident
  instruction and subsequently perform it independently. Player-only teaching
  cannot replace that particular proof; limited inherited capacity stays limited.

The first Living Keystone requires actual evidence of renewable wood/food,
transport, shared meals, resident-made grafting frames, paid shelter work,
autonomous courtship, nurturing/hatching, and that independent taught offspring.
Stocking the chest, adding residents or waiting alone cannot qualify it.

Four paid graft-tending operations then produce one physical Living Keystone:
each spends eight guavas, two bone meal and one frame, retaining a food reserve.
The tree shows roots, supports and luminous fruit as it develops. Output present
pauses further Keystone production; taking it permits another paid cultivation
cycle. Completed work and partial tending persist; shortages/unloads do not
reset them. Losing shelter blocks pauses completion until repaired.

## Ritual integration and old items

Install a Keystone once in the Apex Crucible. Its actual item/components are
stored in the block entity; breaking the Crucible returns it. Summons consume
one Hearth Incense, one chosen Tissue Sample and, when selected, one role sigil.
The installed Keystone survives every summon and failed fight. Incense is
renewable from plain guavas and planks. The native explicit review/confirmation,
exact held-source/focus checks, permissions, spawn rejection and reentry guards
remain. Costs are paid only after spawn callbacks revalidate the installed heart
and current supplies. Pedestals and five rare animal deaths are no longer needed.

Existing legendary items/IDs remain keepsakes and each can optionally be crafted
into sixty-four incense. Existing displays/stock are untouched. Old Crucibles
can receive a new heart. No command, recipe or chest grants a free Survival
Keystone. The first household uses no Apex/dimension/dungeon ingredient.

## Six personal hunt resonances

Every ground role is distinct: Brute, Prismmaw, Broodcaller, Blastmaw and Lifebinder.
A flying kill earns only the sixth aerial mark, regardless of its role. Duplicate
roles do not replace missing ground marks. Records are world-owned by player
UUID and survive death, reconnects and dimension travel. No inventory item loan
or editable tooltip is authorization. Existing history without distinct evidence
is not silently treated as all six hunts.

Each ordinary Apex saves bounded contribution scores (up to 32 players). Actual
post-damage health damage, a keeper's companion damage and blocked incoming
Apex damage contribute. Actual healing of another engaged contributor earns
support. The pre-Apex Hearth Poultice provides six accepted HP of ally healing
with a five-second cooldown; canceled healing refunds it. Concord Standards
also credit accepted support when later available. Self-healing, idle proximity
and merely dealing the final hit are not independent entitlements. At a confirmed
uncanceled death, contributors with at least max(6 HP, 2% boss max health) earn
the mark. Killing-hit contribution is included. Crown encounters/brood are
excluded. Native post-death/tick resolution is idempotent.

A Crest reads the holder's authoritative hunt history on normal use; sneak-use
retains its existing source inspection. Its tooltip carries a small synced
summary. Using an Anchor lights the six physical colored inlays for that
interacting player. The Anchor reports a short count; deliberate Crest inspection names the missing
roles in naturally wrapped chat, avoiding an overflowing action-bar list.

Both first attunement and using an already linked entry require all six personal
marks. A Crest and Eye are still the physical activation items. Empty-handed
return travel from the Great Warren is always available, including for an
unqualified stranded player; saved counterpart positions remain unchanged.

## Scope and verification

This delivers the society-to-first-Apex-to-six-hunts-to-realm loop. It does not
claim arbitrary building plans, universal professions, general observed player
demonstrations, long-distance taught courier programs, independent wild-town
bootstrap, inter-town trade or the complete I2–I10 roadmap. The household uses
one explicitly authorized plot, bounded linked sites and real native actions.

Native scenarios cover a complete paid household/offspring/Keystone chain,
real native courtship/seed/cradle/pupil knowledge and persistence, unsupervised
native walking/crafting AI, staff dispatch through animal and composter use,
protection-veto refunds, exact cargo/death drops, stored-inventory destruction,
real hunt deaths/co-op healing, borrowed-Crest and linked-entry rejection, and
unqualified emergency return. The detailed chain fast-forwards personal work
rests, crop maturity and juvenile growth; it does not establish natural pacing.
Existing native worldgen/ProtoChunk and accepted fight suites remain active.
Original art and native gray container UI are headless-checked; first-person
readability, natural household pacing and prolonged Survival/client/co-op play
remain player review, rather than claims inferred from test counts.
