# Great Warren treasure and material projects

**Accepted design history, implemented 2026-10-28.**
[LANTERN_WARRENS_AND_GUILD_PROJECTS.md](LANTERN_WARRENS_AND_GUILD_PROJECTS.md)
records the shipped recipes, exact expanded bills, physical projects, paid
trading/teleport towns and whole-site reward splits. Its quantities supersede
the provisional capital costs below. The implementation keeps the whole-site
material/vanilla budgets proposed here. The enchantment collection remains a
separate planning-only proposal in DUNGEON_ENCHANTMENTS_PLAN.md; the real binding
bench currently uses existing compatible books.

## What coming home with treasure should mean

The current vault's 32–48 Netherite blocks, 64 diamond blocks and 128 enchanted
apples overshoot the useful vanilla scale. Replacing a handful of arrows with
enormous vanilla wealth did not give the dimension an economy of its own.

A successful expedition should give me a wagon-load of useful material. Back
home I can armour several companions, supply my next expeditions, construct a
defensible outpost, or start a caravan. Those are different projects with visible
results. Vanilla food/XP/mobility are travel supplies; a Core, specialist book
and genetic archive remain distinct prizes. Neither raw volume nor a block of
Netherite should substitute for those rewards.

Use **hundreds of raw materials per wing and thousands across a complete raid**.
Avoid a new universal token, flat damage multiplier, meaningless ingot tiers or
stacks of ingredients with no implemented use.

## Materials worth bringing home

| Material | Main demand | Intended source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Astralite Shards | Armour plating, mechanisms, high-grade repair | Rich moons first; earned post-trial/finale foundry stock later |
| Raw Cinderite / Cinderite | Physical frames, ammunition, optional station fuel | Moon mining and substantial Furnace/freight stores |
| Moonstone | Existing lunar masonry, foundations and project bodies | Bulk mining and architectural material stores |
| Starlit Dust | Optical/volatile refining, enchantment work, infrastructure | Observatory stores and existing natural production |
| Sinew | Armour repairs, reinforced harnesses and mechanisms | Existing drops and finite nursery/armoury stores |
| Whiskers | Optical/sensor components in modest quantities | Existing sources and specialist store rolls |
| **Root Fibre (new proposal)** | Bulk cloth, straps, construction, nursery bedding | Deliberately process one Guava Log into four fibres; later workers can use that ordinary paid recipe |
| Role parts / Apex Marrow / membranes | Distinct catalysts, equipment, combat supply | Actual Apex kills and their original reward ownership |

Root Fibre is the only proposed new raw material. Existing minerals, Dust and
animal materials do most of the work. No extra ore dimension or five powders
just to dilute loot. Bulk civic construction uses plant fibre; Sinew/Whiskers
serve reinforcement and precision jobs. Legendary **Moon Sinew** is not ordinary
Sinew and must not become a common bale. Everblooms, Listening Shells, source
Crests and ground remnants retain their origin and purpose.

The first Astralite pick/set still comes from the pre-dungeon moon loop. Large
shard shipments belong behind completed wings/finale, not an unlocked gatehouse
barrel. This is a reward-location/resource distinction, not a player-data check
requiring someone to own/wear a full set. Skilled early raids remain possible.

## Projects that absorb real quantities

### Equip and maintain an actual pack

An existing honest sink is substantial: upgrading **one existing Netherite
barding** needs 144 Shards, 180 raw Cinderite, 36 Moonstone, four Marrow, six
Lenses, three Membranes, four diamonds and one extra Netherite Ingot through
plating/template crafts. Six companions therefore need **864 Shards, 1,080 raw
Cinderite and 216 Moonstone**, plus catalysts. This is separate from the player's
four-piece armour set; the animals keep their actual genes.

Propose a visible **Pack Armoury** rack: 128 Moonstone, 64 Cinderite, 64 Fibre
and four Marrow. It holds actual suits and repair stock. A paid Astralite repair
batch uses one Shard, two Cinderite and four Sinew for up to 120 missing durability,
with lower-tier alternatives. No health refill from equipping, copied suits or
free reset to perfect condition. Native anvil/Mending remain valid alternatives.
The first version is player operated; a learned armourer is a later profession
integration, not a free skill granted by placing the rack.

The result is a visible equipped expedition pack and supplies to keep it working.
Jobs, inherited limits and training still matter. The rack does not make every
animal intelligent or obedient.

### Supply expeditions through a real workshop

Add optional **bulk mod-material recipes**, retaining existing early recipes and
accepted weapon power/cooldown/wear/per-shot costs:

| Proposed batch | Inputs as raw equivalents | Output/use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prism battery | Shell: 128 Moonstone, 64 Cinderite, 64 Fibre, 18 Whiskers, 9 Shards, one Lens. Recharge: 18 Cinderite + 36 Dust | One physical 512-unit battery; each paid recharge adds up to 256 Cell-equivalent units |
| Volatile magazine | Shell: 64 Moonstone, 64 Cinderite, 32 Fibre, one Volatile Heart. Refill: 18 Cinderite, 18 Fibre, 18 Dust, one Volatile Heart | A finite 128-unit magazine; each refill adds up to 64 Charge-equivalent units. Launcher remains power 3/6/9/12/15 |
| Concord field dressings | 36 Cinderite, 18 Sinew, 18 Dust, one Heart Spool | Four **new processed dressings**, each equivalent to one Standard fuel unit; never cloned Heart Spool organs |

The expensive capital shells reduce carried ammo slots without changing damage.
Existing plain Cells/Charges stay valid; current recipes remain an early route.
A different expensive recipe for the same vanilla-centred ammo would offer no
reason to spend the rarer mod stock. Lances/flight/optical enchants and launcher
modes consume the exact same 1–5 equivalent units from these finite reservoirs.
Each reservoir is one nonstackable item with native saved charge, not a stack
whose shared component accidentally drains every copy. Never pay for unused
recharge capacity, recharge for free on reload or duplicate units when splitting
payment between ordinary ammo and a reservoir.

Catalysts make kill loot useful, while repeat refining burns chest stocks. Direct
Spool fuel remains supported. Dressings must not count as role remnants, craft
an Eye or convert back into organs. Whiskers are a capital optical component rather than a compulsory recurring
ammo expense.

These are **multi-stage recipe bills**, not a grid consuming whole stacks per
slot. Reuse nine-Shard Concentrates, nine-Cinderite Billets and Compressed
Moonstone. Add nine-unit fibre rolls/Dust pouches only where a real native recipe
needs them. Actual recipes let household manufacturing and physical hauling
participate without inventing stock or granting professions automatically.

### Establish useful fortified outposts

Existing lunar masonry is a large practical outlet: bridges, roofed animal yards,
warehouses and landing pads consume hundreds/thousands of actual blocks. Blast
resistance makes that investment worthwhile in this dimension.

Propose a **Bastion Heart** upgrade to an actual Warren Ward: 64 Shards, 128
Cinderite, 256 Moonstone, 32 Dust, four Chorus Hearts and four Spools. Its powered
mode expands *natural-spawn suppression* to 48 blocks. The existing unpowered
Ward effect remains. Active Apexes, projectiles and dungeon waves are not erased,
stunned or stopped by an invisible boundary.

Powered operation burns one Cinderite per loaded minute and one Dust per ten
loaded minutes from a real inventory. One Cinderite stack supplies about an hour
of active operation. No offline catch-up bill, forced chunks or hidden currency.
Learned haulers can replenish stock through their real walking routes. A lasting
forward base does not remove the danger between bases.

Pair it later with a **launch aerie**: 128 Moonstone, 64 Cinderite, 64 Fibre,
eight Membranes and four Lenses. A paid updraft (two Cinderite plus one Dust)
helps a player with actual Elytra gain height. It does not teleport a party
between dungeons, grant wingless animals flight or move saved return portals.

### Move a real caravan through the tunnels

Propose a visible **kennel wagon**, pulled by a sufficiently strong trained
bunnidog: 128 Cinderite, 192 Fibre, 32 Sinew, 64 Moonstone, four Membranes and two
Chorus Hearts. Four actual companion berths and a finite 27-slot freight inventory
let the player take an organised pack expedition. Gear/cargo remain visible and
owned; actual animal saves preserve health, genes, training and equipment.

Start with player-driven travel on roads, bridges and tunnels. Heavy freight
trades speed for capacity. Ground animals do not acquire aerial mobility;
unloaded travel pauses. No cloned seeds, hostile Apex capture, taking another
keeper's pet or teleporting parcels. Autonomous inter-community caravans depend
on unfinished learning/civilization work; the wagon alone does not complete it.

### Build a working nursery district

Propose a physically built **nursery hearth** and replaceable Fibre bedding.
A hearth costs 128 Moonstone, 64 Cinderite, 96 Fibre and two Chorus Hearts;
bedding uses Fibre with a little Sinew/Dust. Proper shelter, real meals and
bedding give up to **25% faster loaded-time pup growth** and ordinary comfort.
Multiple hearths do not stack the reduction.

Parents/seeds stay exact. No raised genetic ceiling, guaranteed mutation,
inherited skill, free food or instant adult/civilization. A learned caretaker can
eventually maintain actual bedding/meal stock. Raw treasure expands a living
settlement rather than replacing breeding and education.

### Give treasure books a material workshop

Propose a **resonance binding bench**: 128 Moonstone, 128 Cinderite, 64 Shards,
128 Fibre, 64 Dust and four Lenses. It applies an actual existing book to actual
equipment, using a paid batch of mod materials as an alternative to some anvil
XP/prior-work cost. Consume the book, follow supported items/exclusive sets/levels,
and preserve the item's other components.

It does not discover missing book types, copy a stored book, reroll on reload,
raise enchantment levels or replace ordinary anvils. Per-book prices should
follow the enchantment implementation. Do not introduce a binding currency and
invent uses later. Library stock competes with the other material projects.

## What the chests should look like later

Design the room first, then its table. A nursery contains fibre and care stock;
a furnace looks like a mineral warehouse. Finished weapons, treasure books,
the personal Core and the frozen genetic archive stay separate.

| Storage identity | Proposed material emphasis |
| --- | --- |
| Entry freight/material stores | 128–256 Moonstone, 64–128 Fibre, 64 raw Cinderite; no easy bulk Astralite |
| Glass reading/instrument stores | 32–64 Dust, 16–32 Whiskers, 64 Cinderite; 32–64 Shards only in the earned wing reward |
| Brood nursery/armoury stores | 64–128 Fibre, 32–64 Sinew, 128–256 Moonstone, 64 Cinderite |
| Furnace/assay stores | 128–192 raw Cinderite, 128–256 Moonstone, 32–64 Dust; 64–128 Shards in earned refining stock after its trial |
| Crown foundry vault | 192–256 Shards, 256–512 raw Cinderite, 128 Moonstone, 128 Dust, 256–512 Fibre, 64 Sinew |

The vault example's maximum occupies **25 slots**, before other loot. Do not
append vanilla supplies/books and silently spill the last rolls. Material stores,
recovery supplies and book cabinets need separate capacity budgets/identities.
Each row is the combined budget for that storage identity in a complete site,
not an independent roll to multiply into every decorative barrel. Distribute a
row over actual matching containers with a declared split; optional extra rooms
may change where stock is found, not silently multiply the whole-site budget.

Cinderite stock is counted in raw-equivalent units (smelting is one-to-one),
so refined and raw shipments do not get counted twice.

First target for a complete three-wing/finale haul: **288–448 Shards, 576–896
raw Cinderite, 512–896 Moonstone, 384–768 Fibre, 192–256 Dust, 96–128 Sinew
and 16–32 Whiskers**, plus real combat drops. This supports roughly two to three
companion plating upgrades *or* a substantial infrastructure project, rather than
every project from one visit. Budget representative complete sites before coding;
Looting fewer rooms gives a smaller haul; decoration count must not multiply it. The baseline must not rely on empty-hand
luck. Repeat journeys can fund an armoury, a caravan and a network over time.

Vanilla recovery/treasure target **across the whole complete site**, not per
barrel: about **32–64 golden apples, 4–8 enchanted apples, 4–6 Totems, 8–16
diamonds and 1–3 Netherite Ingots**. Add modest XP/pearls/Flight-1 rockets in
actual recovery rooms. No routine Netherite/diamond/gold block piles. The mod
projects, books, gear and Core carry the exceptional reward. Ordinary-world farms
and all kit benchmarks are independent; do not nerf them in this loot pass.

## Implement later in this order

1. Prototype real refining recipes and Pack Armoury against current items,
   including native grid shapes, wear, enchants and stock payment. Do not raise
   existing armour costs again merely to create sinks.
2. Introduce Fibre through a paid Guava Log conversion and actual inventories.
   Keep recipe-book discovery and existing household material authority.
3. Ship visible projects in stages: armoury, outpost, then wagon. Nursery and book
   binding depend on their real systems, not placeholder UI.
4. Update **tables and their art/data generators together**. Assign them to actual
   room containers, enforce whole-site vanilla/material budgets and every native
   stack/slot maximum. Sample complete site inventories, not a handpicked barrel.
5. Release new loot with its first useful material sinks. Stacks with no working
   use repeat the problem. Never refill opened containers, reroll archives or
   rewrite saved stock. The original finite trial/Core journals stay authoritative.

Use ordinary physical storage and reversible nine-unit compact blocks for a
fixed whitelist of fungible materials. Never compress arbitrary gene seeds,
samples, origin-bearing role parts or named/component-bearing items into generic
counts. No personal completion checks replace physical gates. Jobs pause unloaded;
no project may erase a stranded parcel, grant unsupported minds knowledge or
force-load a route. Test native payment/cancellation/reload/full-stock cases when
features ship; headless transaction proof remains separate from Survival pacing.
